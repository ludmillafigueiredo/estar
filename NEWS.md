# estar 2.0-0

## Major fixes

* `asympt_resil` does not assume the `B` matrix is symmetric.

* In the `Jacobian stability properties` vignette, Z scores are estimated from
the log of abundances values, as it should be for Multivariate Autoregressive 
State-Space models estimated by the `MARSS` package.

## Minor fixes

* The Jacobian matrix `B` is more thoroughly described and illustrated in the 
`Jacobian stability properties` vignette.
